About Safina Initiative

Every Community
Deserves to Thrive

Founded with a simple but powerful belief — that access to education, healthcare, and opportunity should not depend on where you were born — Safina Initiative has spent over a decade walking alongside communities across Kenya to build change that truly lasts.

Community-Led Programs

Designed with and for the people we serve — not delivered to them.
