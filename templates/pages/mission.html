Our Mission

What We Set Out to Do

The Safina Initiative provides quality healthcare and education to vulnerable rural populations in the Rift Valley, Kenya. We pursue this mission by working closely with local communities to build, operate, and sustain health care facilities and schools underserved rural communities

Education

Ensuring every child has access to quality learning and a safe environment to grow.

Healthcare

Providing access to basic healthcare services and health education for all.

Opportunity

Creating economic pathways that lift families and strengthen entire communities.
